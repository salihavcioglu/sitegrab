FAQ

      

        

          What ends up in the ZIP?
          
Every HTML page reached within your depth limit, plus the CSS, JavaScript, images (including srcset), fonts, favicons and media posters they reference. CSS url() and @import are followed too.

        

        

          Does it follow links to other websites?
          
No. The crawl stays on the host you paste. Assets hosted elsewhere, such as on a CDN, are still downloaded so pages render.

        

        

          What do depth and max pages mean?
          
Depth 0 grabs only the pasted page. Depth 1 also grabs pages it links to, and so on up to 5. Max pages is a hard cap regardless of depth; max size stops the crawl once that many megabytes have been fetched.

        

        

          Why was my download refused or stopped early?
          
Private, local and internal addresses are blocked. There is also a limit on jobs per hour and on concurrent jobs. Sites that block bots or require a login will not come through fully.

        

        

          Will JavaScript-rendered pages work offline?
          
HTML is saved as served, not as a browser-rendered snapshot. Sites that build everything client-side may look empty offline; server-rendered sites work best.

        

        

          How long is the ZIP kept?
          
About 30 minutes after it is created. Download it right away.
